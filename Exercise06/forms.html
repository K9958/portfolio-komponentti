Conclusion

Bootstrap forms is a lot easier to make with ready-made snippets and its ready made classes. But if you need to customize bootstrap forms, it is sligtly easier to do it with your custom CSS, unless you are very familiar with bootstrap from previous experiences. There are so many different classes that will help you if you know them beforehand.

The benefits of bootstrap is the uniformity of its styles and the ease of use. The downside is that it is not as customizable as your own CSS.
